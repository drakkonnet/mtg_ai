# Commander/EDH Analysis Checklist

Use this as a compact review checklist.

## Legality
- Commander is legal and matches any partner/background/doctor companion rules.
- 100 cards exactly including commander(s).
- No duplicate non-basic cards.
- All cards match commander color identity, including hybrid mana, color indicators, rules text mana symbols, and back faces.
- No banned cards.
- No cards from non-legal product categories unless Rule 0 is explicitly allowed.

## Functional shell
- Mana base supports color needs and early plays.
- Ramp count matches curve and commander cost.
- Draw engines recover after removal and wipes.
- Interaction answers creatures, artifacts/enchantments, stacks/combo pieces, and graveyards where colors allow.
- Win plan is explicit and has redundancy.
- Protection exists for commander-centric strategies.

## Common red flags
- Too many tapped lands in a deck trying to play fast.
- Too many six-plus mana cards without ramp or cheat effects.
- Theme cards that do not draw, ramp, remove, tutor, protect, or win.
- Single combo line with no tutors or backup plan.
- High average mana value with too few early plays.
- Cards that are powerful in 60-card formats but weak in multiplayer Commander.
