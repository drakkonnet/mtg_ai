# Budget Upgrade Rules

Use these rules when recommending purchases or replacements.

## Price bands
- Budget: usually under $3.
- Efficient budget: usually under $10 and materially improves the deck.
- Midrange: roughly $10-$25; recommend only with a clear role.
- Premium: above $25; mention as optional unless the user asks for best-in-slot.

## Selection policy
1. Prefer owned cards when collection data is available.
2. Prefer cheap cards that solve structural problems before splashy threats.
3. Prefer recent printings when they are similar in power and cost.
4. Do not recommend expensive fast mana or free interaction by default for casual brackets.
5. Separate “best functional buy” from “luxury upgrade” when relevant.

## Upgrade order
1. Legal fixes.
2. Mana base and ramp.
3. Card draw and selection.
4. Interaction and protection.
5. Win condition redundancy.
6. Tutors and premium optimization.
